6. Key Findings
29%
ACTIVE INCIDENT: Sharing notification loader spiked to 29%
ACTIONABLE_NOTIFICATION_DETAILS (the loader that fires when users tap a sharing-request push notification) rose from 8% on 9 May to 29% on 1–2 Jun 2026. This sits directly on the sharing-request critical path. A total of 415K infinite-loader events in the capture window. Dropped to 6% on 3–4 Jun — cause unknown, requires root-cause analysis immediately.
20%
eSeal / Evidence fetch is the most severe persistent loader
ISSUED_DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE averages 20.4% infinite-loader rate across the full 44-day period (477K events >10s). This loader fires on every document opened by a user. Android is 25.6% vs iOS 11.6% — a 14pp platform gap. No clear improvement trend across versions v6.5–v6.7.
12.5%
Document detail screen: 392K infinite-loader events
ISSUED_DOCUMENT_DETAILS (the main doc viewer screen) has 12.5% infinite loaders — 392K events in period. Notably improved from 25% in v6.5.1 to 10% in v6.7.0 (a positive signal from recent releases). Still 3.1M total events make this the second-largest absolute slow-loader source.
34%
Document selection (sharing flow) has highest rate — small volume
DOCUMENT_SELECTION_UPLOADED_LIST hits 34% infinite loaders; DOCUMENT_SELECTION_ISSUED_LIST hits 26%. These fire during the sharing consent flow when users select documents. Low absolute volume (335 and 4,781 events) but the severity is a signal of structural latency in the sharing pipeline.
15.8%
SHARE_DOCUMENT loader: consent-to-delivery POST is slow
SHARE_DOCUMENT (the verifiable-presentation delivery call) is 15.8% infinite loaders — 3,131 out of 19,871 sharing attempts hit a >10s wait. This is a direct contributor to sharing-request conversion failure (complements the known 300-series consent drop-off). iOS and Android are near-parity here (~16%).
6.1%
System-wide: 1.71M infinite-loader events in 44 days
Of 28.1M loader events, 6.1% took longer than 10 seconds. The additional 5–10s band (SLOW but not infinite) adds another ~5% in some screens. The combined 5–10s + 10s+ bucket on ISSUED_DOCUMENT_EVIDENCE is 37%, meaning over one-third of evidence loads are measurably degraded user experiences.
